4. BLOCKCHAIN EVIDENCE VAULTING

The admissibility of blockchain-anchored evidence varies by jurisdiction and is subject to the rules of evidence applicable in each court. Judges and courts have broad discretion regarding the admission of digital evidence. Alibi Protocol makes no representation that vaulted evidence will be accepted in any legal proceeding.
