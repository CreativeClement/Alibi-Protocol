🔒 YOUR DATA — WHAT HAPPENS TO IT
✓
Recordings never leave your device
All audio and video is stored locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server without your explicit action.
✓
Hashing is done on your device
SHA-256 is computed locally using the Web Crypto API. Only the hash is shown in your vault — not the recording itself. The hash is not sent anywhere.
✓
GPS stays local
Location is used only for state detection, speed display, and evidence packaging. Your GPS track is stored in memory only — cleared when you close the app.
ℹ
AI guidance uses our secure proxy
When you tap ASK AI, your state and selected scenario are sent to our Cloudflare Worker proxy, which calls the Claude API. No recording audio or personal identifiers are ever sent.
ℹ
Founding Shield stores wallet + email only
If you claim a Founding Shield, your Solana wallet address and optional email are saved to our secure database to reserve your token allocation. Nothing else.
