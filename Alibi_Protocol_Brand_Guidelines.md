## Executive Summary

Alibi Protocol is the first blockchain-based evidence preservation network for everyday drivers. We exist at the intersection of civil rights technology and decentralized infrastructure — a place where cryptographic certainty replaces he-said/she-said. Our brand must communicate that seriousness without becoming cold, that urgency without becoming alarmist, and that technical depth without becoming inaccessible.

The Alibi Protocol voice is the voice of the **Guardian Engineer**: someone who has thought deeply about the problem, built something precise to solve it, and trusts you to understand why it matters.

## We Are / We Are Not

| We Are | We Are Not |
|--------|-----------|
| **Authoritative** — We speak from conviction backed by engineering. We do not need to raise our voice. | **Preachy** — We don't moralize or lecture. We state facts and let users draw conclusions. |
| **Urgent** — Every drive is an unprotected moment. We communicate the real cost of inaction. | **Alarmist** — We don't manufacture fear or exaggerate threat. The stakes are already real. |
| **Technical** — We respect precision. Specs are features. Cryptographic proofs are promises. | **Jargon-heavy** — We explain what matters and cut what doesn't. Complexity serves clarity, not gatekeeping. |
| **Defiant** — We were built in response to a broken system. That origin is part of our DNA. | **Radical** — We are not anti-institution. We are pro-accountability. We build tools, not manifestos. |
| **Transparent** — Every transaction, every burn, every split — all on-chain and verifiable. | **Vague** — We never use hedge words to obscure commitments. If we can't verify it, we don't claim it. |
| **Military-precise** — Our UX and copy are tight, structured, and deliberate. Every word earns its space. | **Cold** — Precision is warm when it's in service of protecting someone. We care about the humans involved. |

## Brand Personality — The Guardian Engineer Archetype

The Guardian Engineer doesn't need to convince you something bad could happen. They've already built the system for when it does.

**Core traits:**
- Speaks with the economy of someone who has already done the math
- Builds for edge cases because edge cases are where people get hurt
- Prefers showing over telling — receipts over promises
- Takes the problem seriously so the user doesn't have to carry that weight alone
- Assumes intelligence in the audience; explains process, not intent

**The archetype in practice:**
> A civil rights attorney walks into a dashcam store. They're also a Solana validator operator. They built something because they got tired of watching cases fall apart due to evidence tampering.

That's the brand voice. Not hype. Not fear. Precision in service of protection.

## Messaging Framework

### Primary Message
> **"Your encounter. Your evidence. Your chain of custody."**

This is the core. Three parallel possessives. Three escalating stakes. It lives on the hero section, in ads, in investor decks, and in any context where we have one line to define ourselves.

### The Four Pillars

**1. Immutable by Design**
Evidence is cryptographically sealed the moment it's recorded. Not editable. Not deletable. Not subject to someone else's version of events.

*Supporting language:* "SHA-256 fingerprinted at capture." / "Tamper-evident. On-chain. Permanent." / "If it happened, it's preserved."

**2. Yours, Not Theirs**
Private by default. Encrypted. You control access. You decide if and when it's shared, subpoenaed, or sold.

*Supporting language:* "Your data. Your keys." / "Opt-in to the marketplace. Opt-out at any time." / "We built the vault. You hold the combination."

**3. Revenue-Backed Scarcity**
10% base burn rate (governance-adjustable up to 20%) of all protocol revenue is used to buy $ALIBI from the open market and permanently burn it. The protocol earns → the token becomes scarcer. Burn accelerates automatically at growth milestones: 10K DAU → 12%, 50K DAU → 15%, 100K DAU → 18%. No arbitrary burn targets. No inflation.

*Supporting language:* "The more the protocol earns, the scarcer the token becomes." / "Revenue-proportional. On-chain. Verifiable." / "Scarcity earned, not manufactured." / "Growth compounds burn."

**4. Network Effects as Protection**
Every driver who joins extends the coverage map. More coverage = higher data value = more revenue = more burn = greater token scarcity. The Growth Fund reinvests 10% of revenue into user acquisition, compounding this flywheel. The network funds its own growth, then burns the proceeds.

*Supporting language:* "Your node strengthens everyone's alibi." / "Coverage begets coverage." / "The network remembers what institutions forget."

## Tone-by-Context Matrix

| Context | Tone | Register | Key Techniques |
|---------|------|----------|----------------|
| **Hero / Landing Page** | Confident, declarative, slightly cinematic | Short sentences. Present tense. No qualifiers. | Rule of three. Parallel structure. Single powerful claim per block. |
| **Investor / Pitch Deck** | Precise, data-forward, credibility-first | Numbers before nouns. Proof before promise. | Specific metrics. On-chain citations. Market sizing from bottom up. |
| **Technical / Whitepaper** | Rigorous, architectural, peer-review quality | Define before use. Diagrams over descriptions. | Protocol specs, hash formats, validator economics. No hand-waving. |
| **Legal / Disclaimer** | Neutral, clear, no creative embellishment | Plain language where possible. No jargon. | Active voice even in legal context. User-legible, not just lawyer-legible. |
| **Community / Social** | Warmer, still sharp, never cringe | Short. Pointed. Occasionally dry. | Lead with the hook. Cut everything else. One idea per post. |
| **Product / App UI** | Functional, calm, action-oriented | Verb-led microcopy. Status language. | "Recording active." "Vault sealed." No system errors, only clear next steps. |
| **Crisis / Incident** | Steady, transparent, immediate | Facts first. Speculation never. | What happened. What we know. What we're doing. Timeline. |

## Terminology Guide

### Must-Use (Always preferred)

| Preferred Term | Why |
|----------------|-----|
| Evidence vault | Our core differentiator — implies security, permanence, legal weight |
| Chain of custody | Legal language that validates our use case; users recognize it |
| Buy-and-burn | Active, mechanical — describes what actually happens |
| Proof-of-Coverage-Navigation (PoCN) | Our proprietary consensus mechanism — always capitalize, always spell out on first use |
| $ALIBI | The token ticker — always with dollar sign, always caps |
| Encounter | Neutral, precise — covers incidents, stops, accidents, disputes |
| Sealed | For evidence that's been cryptographically finalized |
| On-chain | Always hyphenated |

### Preferred (Use in most contexts)

- "Revenue-proportional" not "revenue-tied" or "revenue-linked"
- "Permanently burn" not "destroy" or "remove from circulation"
- "Tamper-evident" not "tamper-proof" (precision matters — we detect tampering, not prevent reality)
- "Cryptographically sealed" not "secured" (vague) or "locked" (implies unlock)
- "Verification node" not "validator" (validator is Solana-specific; we have our own layer)

### Avoid

- "Hack-proof" — nothing is hack-proof; say "cryptographically resistant" or cite the specific protection
- "Revolutionary" — overused; let the tech speak
- "Game-changer" — same
- "Safe" as a standalone claim — be specific about what kind of safety
- "Simple" — if it were simple, someone else would have done it; say "streamlined" or describe the actual UX
- "Blockchain technology" as a standalone term — name the chain (Solana) and why it matters

### Never Use

- Any language that implies we are anti-police, anti-government, or anti-institution
- "Big Brother" framing (makes us sound conspiratorial)
- "Nothing to hide" (insults users who have legitimate privacy concerns)
- "Disrupting" (startup cliché with no signal value)
- Any promise of legal outcome (we preserve evidence; we do not guarantee verdicts)
- "Anonymous" (we are private, not anonymous — important legal and technical distinction)

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| Background | Void Black | `#0A0A0A` | Primary backgrounds, deep containers |
| Primary Accent | Signal Cyan | `#00F0FF` | CTAs, data highlights, active states, key metrics |
| Secondary Accent | Proof Green | `#00FF88` | Confirmed states, success indicators, "sealed" status |
| Warning / Attention | Evidence Amber | `#FF9F0A` | Caution states, vesting schedules, time-sensitive info |
| Alert / Critical | Alert Red | `#FF3B30` | Burn events, critical alerts, irreversible actions |

**Contrast rule:** Signal Cyan (`#00F0FF`) and Proof Green (`#00FF88`) must always appear on `#0A0A0A` or near-black backgrounds. Never on white. Never on light gray.

**Background texture:** Dark surfaces may use subtle dot-grid or scanline overlays at ≤4% opacity. Do not use gradient meshes or light color washes — they undermine the precision aesthetic.

### Typography

**Primary — JetBrains Mono**
- Use for: All technical content, metrics, token amounts, hash strings, code blocks, terminal-style UI, percentage callouts, labels
- Weight variants: 400 (body code), 700 (emphasis), 900 (hero numerics)
- Never use in italic for primary brand context

**Secondary — Inter**
- Use for: Body copy, paragraph text, navigation, legal copy, investor prose
- Weight variants: 400 (body), 500 (interface labels), 600 (subheadings), 700 (section headers), 900 (display)
- Line height: 1.6 for prose, 1.2 for display

**Type scale (display contexts):**
- Hero numerics: `clamp(48px, 8vw, 120px)`, weight 900, JetBrains Mono
- Section headers: `clamp(32px, 4vw, 56px)`, weight 700, Inter
- Subheadings: `clamp(18px, 2.5vw, 24px)`, weight 600, Inter
- Body: 16–18px, weight 400, Inter, line-height 1.6

### UI Patterns

**Glass cards:** `background: rgba(255,255,255,0.03)` + `border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.07)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` — for metric containers and feature blocks.

**Monospace data blocks:** Terminal-style containers with `background: rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`, `border-left: 3px solid var(--cyan)`, `font-family: JetBrains Mono`. Use for technical specs, protocol parameters, on-chain data.

**CTA buttons:** Solid Signal Cyan fill (`#00F0FF`) with `#0A0A0A` text. Secondary CTAs: transparent with cyan border and cyan text. Never use white or gray CTAs.

**Status indicators:** Animated dot + label. Active recording: Proof Green pulsing dot. Evidence sealed: Proof Green static. Alert: Alert Red pulse.

**Spacing system:** 8px base unit. Components at 24/32/40/48/64/80px. Section padding: `clamp(80px, 10vw, 140px)` vertical.

**Motion:** Subtle. Ease-out on entrances. No bouncing. No particle systems on primary UI. Scanline overlays and data flickers are acceptable as ambient background texture only.

## Content Examples

### On-Brand

**Hero copy:**
> Your encounter. Your evidence. Your chain of custody.
>
> Alibi Protocol seals your dashcam footage to the Solana blockchain the moment it's recorded — cryptographically fingerprinted, tamper-evident, and legally defensible. Not if something happens. For when it does.

**Tokenomics callout:**
> 10% base burn rate, governance-adjustable to 20%. All protocol revenue — Data Marketplace, Premium Features, API Access, Insurance Partnerships — fuels a buy-and-burn. Milestones accelerate it. Revenue-proportional scarcity — no arbitrary targets. The more the protocol earns, the scarcer the token becomes.

**Feature description:**
> **Evidence Vault**
> Every file sealed with SHA-256. Hash written to Solana at capture time. Immutable. Your attorney can verify it. Their attorney cannot dispute it.

**Social post:**
> Evidence without chain of custody is just footage.
> We seal it at capture. On-chain. Permanent.
> $ALIBI

### Off-Brand

**Do not write:**
> "🚨 REVOLUTIONIZING how you PROTECT yourself from corrupt cops!!! Get in early before this 100x's!!! 🚀🚀🚀"

*Why it's wrong:* Alarmist framing, anti-institution positioning, hype language, emoji overuse, price speculation.

**Do not write:**
> "Our blockchain technology provides a simple, safe, game-changing solution that disrupts the traditional dashcam industry."

*Why it's wrong:* "Blockchain technology" is vague; "simple" undersells complexity; "safe" is unspecific; "game-changing" and "disrupts" are clichés; "traditional dashcam industry" is the wrong competitive frame.

**Do not write:**
> "With Alibi Protocol, you'll have nothing to worry about when dealing with law enforcement."

*Why it's wrong:* "Nothing to worry about" is a legal outcome promise we cannot make; it trivializes user safety concerns.
